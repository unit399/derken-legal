AI statement

Last updated: October 24, 2026

AI tools are used in writing Derken’s stories. We do not hide it; this page explains where and how.

Credits

Designed, written and edited by Okan Çilingiroğlu. AI tools were used during drafting.

How we write

  • A story’s idea, world, characters and rules are human decisions.
  • Large language models (Anthropic’s Claude and Google’s Gemini) write the first drafts of chapters, to an approved plan.
  • Every draft goes through automatic checks: the page and choice rules, dead ends, and thousands of random readings.
  • The final text, its voice and its rhythm pass through human hands; a person is responsible for every published sentence.
  • The English editions are also drafted with AI and reviewed by a person.

Where AI is not used

  • The covers are not made with AI; they are public domain artworks from museum and library collections.
  • No AI runs inside the app. The stories are written in advance: nothing is generated while you read, and your choices are not sent to a language model.

Software

AI coding tools are also used in building the app’s software.

Why we say so

Readers have a right to know what they are reading. We make no claim such as “100% human-written”: we use AI as a drafting tool and leave the last word to a person.
